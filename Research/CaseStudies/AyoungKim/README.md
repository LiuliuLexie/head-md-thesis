# Ayoung Kim
- Artist Website: [link](http://ayoungkim.com/wp/)

I went to her exhibition [*Many Worlds Over*](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/hamburger-bahnhof/exhibitions/detail/ayoung-kim/) in Hamburger Bahnhof in April 20, 2025. In the exhibition, Kim exhibits a series of works she did around the story of a female delivery driver and her identical double in a fictional, futuristic Seoul. In the "Delivery Dancer" universe, infinite possible worlds collide, where time is cyclical and non-linear. (same world setting but "multiple" "worlds" because the subject crosses the boundaries of realities, creating intersections between times and spaces. By using different media forms in the same topic and narration, and the exhibition space, I really feel getting immersed in the project.
